# NON-GOALS

What this repo will never become. Each of these has killed a repo like this one.

**Not an awesome-list.** No collections of links to other people's content. If a fact is needed at 3am it lives in a file here, not behind a click.

**Not your runbook.** Nothing here knows your service names, your dashboards, or your on-call rotation. It starts one step earlier: you are staring at a symptom and cannot decide what to type. If you want it to know your system, fork it and add three lines under each file.

**Not a monitoring or observability guide.** No advice on what to instrument. That advice is useful on a Tuesday afternoon and useless right now.

**Not a teaching resource.** No explanation of how TLS, DNS, or connection pools work. Someone who needs that is not in an incident.

**Not a place for write commands.** No restarts, no rollbacks, no `DROP`, no `kill -9` — not even the obviously correct ones. The guarantee that nothing here changes your system is worth more than any single fix, because it is what makes the repo safe to run before you understand the problem.

**Not exhaustive.** Thirteen symptoms, not one hundred and thirty. A long list is a list you scroll past. New symptoms get in by displacing weak ones, not by piling up.
